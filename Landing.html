About SmartCrop

Technology that works closer to the farm.

SmartCrop is an agriculture-assistance platform designed to bring crop recommendations, weather information, market data and pest advisory tools into one accessible experience.

The platform combines machine learning with agricultural information to help users explore data and make more informed farming decisions.
