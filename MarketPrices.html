🌾 SmartCrop Market Price Tracker

ℹ Modal Price

Modal price is the most common price at which a crop is sold in a mandi on that day.

Mandis record three prices:

Min Price – Lowest price recorded
Max Price – Highest price recorded
Modal Price – Price at which most trades happen

Prices are reported in ₹ per Quintal.
1 Quintal = 100 kg.
